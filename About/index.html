About Dreamy Cake Bakery

Custom cakes, cupcakes & seasonal bakes from Brampton, Huntingdon

Meet Margo

Marharyta “Margo” Makus · Managing Director & Lead Cake Designer

Personal cakes, polished details, and a calm ordering experience.

Hello, I’m Marharyta Makus—Margo to friends and customers—the Managing Director and Lead Cake Designer behind Dreamy Cake Bakery, a made-to-order home bakery in Brampton, Huntingdon. I create celebration cakes, cupcakes and seasonal bakes for birthdays, family milestones, thank-you gifts and the moments people want to make feel a little more special.

My style leans clean, soft and thoughtful rather than overdone. I care just as much about flavour and finish as I do about making the ordering process feel easy: clear replies, realistic guidance, and designs that look lovely in person and are practical to serve.

  • Made to order from my home bakery in Brampton, Huntingdon
  • Celebration cakes, cupcakes and seasonal collections
  • Collection by arrangement, with local delivery available
  • Helpful guidance on portions, flavours and designs

Food hygiene, allergen awareness and a reliable home bakery process

A home bakery clients can feel comfortable ordering from

Alongside design and flavour, I care about the practical side of each order too: safe handling, allergen awareness, tidy collection, and a smooth process from enquiry to handover. The bakery holds relevant training certificates, and the Food Hygiene Rating shown here is 5 — Very Good.
